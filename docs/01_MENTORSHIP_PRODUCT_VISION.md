# Mentorix Mentorship Marketplace — Product Vision

## Executive recommendation

Mentorix should launch a **goal-first, verified mentorship marketplace** rather than a generic tutor directory. The product promise is: **help a learner move from a stated starting point to a measurable outcome through a trusted human guide, a clear plan and accountability**.

Mentorix's existing advantage is not the largest mentor catalog. It is the combination of **Learn + Manage + Grow**: discovery, scheduling, tasks, exams, attendance, messaging, payments and progress can live in one identity and one workspace.

## Product thesis

A tutor sells subject time. A mentor sells structured progress:

- a goal and baseline;
- a roadmap with milestones;
- recurring accountability;
- feedback between sessions;
- evidence of progress;
- a safe, verified relationship.

The primary marketplace object is therefore not a profile alone. It is a **mentor offer** attached to an outcome and a delivery model.

## Product pillars

1. **Outcome-first discovery** — begin with “What do you want to achieve?” and recommend a small set of relevant offers.
2. **Trust by default** — identity, credentials, experience and session history are visible as verified signals.
3. **Structured flexibility** — support one-off consultations, recurring mentorship and milestone-based programs.
4. **Native execution** — booking, messages, tasks, feedback, payments and progress are connected.
5. **Human choice** — matching recommends; the mentee always chooses, requests and can decline.
6. **Local-first, internationally extensible** — AZN and Azerbaijani supply first, with locale/currency/timezone abstractions from day one.

## Target users

### Initial mentee segments

- Azerbaijani university applicants and students needing admissions, language or academic guidance;
- early-career technology learners seeking a first portfolio or job;
- career changers needing a practical roadmap;
- existing Mentorix students who already understand tasks, lessons and progress tracking.

### Initial mentor segments

- verified practicing engineers, data professionals, designers and admissions graduates;
- high-performing Mentorix teachers who want to offer guidance beyond subject lessons;
- Azerbaijani professionals abroad with relevant lived experience;
- selected university/career specialists.

## Recommended launch categories

**Phase 1:** Career & Technology, AI & Data, University & Education, Languages (English/IELTS only). These have clear outcomes, strong content fit, measurable artifacts and plausible local supply.

**Phase 2:** Business, Creative, Professional Skills. **Phase 3:** broader language and specialist categories.

[FOUNDER DECISION REQUIRED] Approve whether minors and school tutoring are included in the first marketplace release. This materially changes safeguarding, consent and support operations.

## North-star metric

**Completed outcome-bearing mentorship engagements per active mentee per quarter**, supported by:

- completed paid sessions;
- goal/milestone progress;
- repeat engagement;
- verified satisfaction;
- low dispute/refund rate.

## What Mentorix should not become

- an unmoderated contact directory;
- a race-to-the-bottom hourly tutoring marketplace;
- a social network with no measurable outcomes;
- a fully custom LMS before marketplace liquidity exists;
- an AI-only “mentor” replacing human accountability.
