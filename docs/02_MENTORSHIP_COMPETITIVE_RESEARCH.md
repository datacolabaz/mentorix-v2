# Competitive Research

## Method and confidence

Research used public product/help pages and current repository context. Prices, commissions and counts change frequently; they are directional, not contractual. Any commercial launch decision requires rechecking provider terms and local legal/tax implications.

## International comparison

| Product | Positioning | Strength | Limitation / opportunity |
|---|---|---|---|
| MentorCruise | Long-term tech/career mentorship | Monthly plans, mentor-set pricing, trial, chat and calls; public help material describes roughly 16.6% global average take rate and one-off fees | Expensive for local learners; outcome/program tooling and local payments are an opportunity |
| ADPList | Global professional mentorship community | Large free network, goal-led entry points, visible social proof and experienced mentors | Paid Advance is selective/limited; free model can create inconsistent availability and weak accountability |
| Preply | Language tutoring marketplace | Strong supply, scheduling, reviews, recurring lessons and acquisition engine | Tutor-style hourly commodity model; trial/commission economics are unattractive to some mentors; weak broader goal programs |
| Superprof | Broad tutor directory | Huge category breadth and local/in-person flexibility | Directory/contact model, variable trust and off-platform payment; not a structured mentorship product |
| Admissions firms / university mentors | High-touch application guidance | Strong domain credibility and outcome-specific consulting | Agency-like, expensive, often not self-serve or scalable marketplace |
| Coding/career coaching platforms | Skill/career outcome coaching | Portfolio and interview outcomes can be measurable | Usually narrow verticals, limited local language/payment support |

### Relevant observed mechanics

- MentorCruise anchors on monthly commitment and lets the mentor set the plan; this validates recurring mentorship but also creates a high price barrier.
- ADPList's free community creates liquidity and trust before paid upgrades; its published Advance criteria show that quality gates can be based on experience, sessions, reviews, attendance and cancellation rate.
- Preply proves demand for scheduling, recurring payments and reviews, but its model optimizes lessons rather than outcomes.
- Superprof shows the scale of a directory but also the trust and leakage risks of off-platform contact/payment.

## Azerbaijan / regional landscape

- **Tutor.az:** teacher directory, admin approval, ratings and direct phone/WhatsApp contact. Strong local discovery; weak payment, progress, program and platform-mediated trust.
- **TutorMap.az:** map-based verified teacher discovery, demo lessons and safe-payment positioning. Strong local UX pattern; primarily tutor/lesson, not mentorship.
- **Sına LMS / iQink:** course/tutor catalog and education commerce. Strong course supply and category breadth; mentorship outcomes and matching are not the central proposition.
- **MentorUp.az:** career mentorship for university students, mentor messaging and matching. Strong proof that focused career mentorship resonates; narrower segment and selection-based.
- **Mentify.az:** international university admissions mentorship from graduates/students. Strong lived-experience trust and admissions specialization; narrower category and likely service-led.
- **FLUX / Career Bridge initiatives:** community/volunteer or paid admissions mentorship for Azerbaijani youth. Strong network and mission; not a general transaction marketplace.

## Differentiation for Mentorix

1. **Goal-to-offer matching**, not search-first profiles.
2. **Three commercial formats**: one-off diagnosis, recurring support and structured programs.
3. **Execution layer**: tasks, milestones, feedback, attendance, messages and evidence connect to the offer.
4. **Verified outcome signals**: completed sessions and program outcomes, not only star ratings.
5. **Local trust and payments**: AZN, Payriff/Epoint adapter, Azerbaijani language and local support.
6. **Existing network advantage**: teachers, students and education centers can become supply/demand without duplicate accounts.

## Competitive risk

Copying Preply's category breadth before liquidity exists would create an empty catalog and a commodity price war. Copying ADPList's free scale without quality operations would create unreliable availability. Copying MentorCruise's premium global pricing would suppress Azerbaijani conversion.

## Strategic conclusion

Launch as a **curated, verified outcome marketplace** in four categories with a small founding cohort. Keep discovery narrow, supply quality high and program execution native. Expand breadth only after repeat booking and completion data proves liquidity.
