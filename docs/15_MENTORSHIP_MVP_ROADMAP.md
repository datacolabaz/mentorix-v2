# MVP Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Curated marketplace MVP

**Goal:** prove that verified supply and goal-led demand produce completed paid engagements.

Include:

- public first-class Mentorluq navigation;
- four categories: Career/Technology, AI & Data, University & Education, English/IELTS;
- 20–50 invite-only verified mentors;
- mentor profiles and one-off/recurring offers;
- goal-first intake plus deterministic recommendations;
- browse fallback;
- availability, booking, timezone, reschedule/cancel;
- Payriff/Epoint adapter decision and one payment path;
- platform commission ledger, manual/initial payout workflow;
- direct chat context, notifications and email/SMS reminders;
- one active relationship dashboard with goals, simple milestones and notes;
- completed-session review;
- admin verification, moderation, refunds, disputes and core analytics;
- AZ/EN/RU keys, with TR/DE fallback handled cleanly.

Exclude from first release: minors, multi-mentor teams, complex cohort billing, AI ranking, native video, full calendar sync, automatic international payouts, broad category catalog and a mentor subscription.

## Phase 2 — Delivery depth

- structured programs with versioned weeks/milestones/tasks;
- recurring billing and grace states;
- Google Calendar integration and external meeting links;
- progress dashboards, mentor feedback and outcome evidence;
- improved search, saved goals, waitlist and referral attribution;
- verified program completion/certificate integration.

## Phase 3 — Marketplace scale

- multilingual mentor supply and currency support;
- advanced matching/pgvector only if needed;
- mentor tiers and Pro tools;
- corporate-sponsored cohorts;
- featured placements with transparent labels;
- automated provider payouts and reconciliation;
- quality scoring and supply coaching.

## Phase 4 — Ecosystem

- mentor-to-mentor community and supervision;
- partner/university integrations;
- B2B reporting/SCIM-like access if justified;
- outcome benchmarking and pathway recommendations;
- additional categories and localized operations.

## Launch gates

Do not expand category count until: at least 70% of approved mentors publish an offer, first response SLA is acceptable, paid booking-to-completion exceeds 80%, refund/dispute rate is controlled, and repeat/renewal signal exists. Exact thresholds are [FOUNDER DECISION REQUIRED].

## Launch strategy

Recruit founding mentors from existing instructors, alumni and trusted Azerbaijani professional communities. Give them profile support, first-booking fee relief and a feedback loop. Demand comes from existing student/teacher network, university partnerships, admissions communities, tech communities, SEO goal pages and referral links. Use concierge matching during the first cohort rather than pretending automation is mature.
